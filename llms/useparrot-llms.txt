# Parrot

> Parrot (useparrot.com) is a Y Combinator-backed startup (Spring 2026 batch) building an agentic, all-in-one management platform for collision and auto repair shops. AI agents automate estimate auditing, parts procurement, insurance claim follow-ups, invoice reconciliation, tax filing, payments, inventory, team coordination, and inbound call handling. It integrates with CCC ONE, Audatex, and Mitchell 1.

Parrot does not currently publish a public API, developer documentation, or client SDKs. The application lives at app.useparrot.com (login required). Footer links to /pricing, /help, /security, /status, and /changelog returned 404 at the time of this snapshot (2026-07-21).
